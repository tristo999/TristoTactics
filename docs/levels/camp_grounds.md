## Intent

One continuous space for the early game: the player **walks out of the summoning room** (south
wall), through a **living camp**, up to the **sparring arena**, where the drill (Beat 2 spar) is
interrupted by a **raid from the forest** (Beat 2 raid). Geometry first, art later.

## Composition

North→south spine: **forest** (raid source / boundary) → **wide sparring arena** → **camp**
(gathering commons) → **summoning wall** (entry). The tactical hook: three forest **clearings**
line up with three **north breaches**, so the raid floods the wide pen down **three lanes** while
the squad defends a broad front with the camp at their back.
