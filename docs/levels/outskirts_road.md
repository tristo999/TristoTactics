## Intent

The first *normal* mission — the war as routine. A brisk, winnable sweep that must feel
**righteous and unremarkable** (Act 1 discipline: no ominous marking). It carries three
firsts: the first **Guardian glitch** (dismissed), the first **moral flicker** (an enemy
reads as frightened for half a beat), and the birth of **bonding v1** (bonds accrue from
how the squad actually fought). It also plants the **cue language** (ledger rule 4): the
player learns "garbled = artifact bleed" cheaply, before it matters in B4.

**Fiction `[GUESS]`:** Throne intel says insurgents seized a waystation on the supply
road out of camp; the squad sweeps it. (Unshown truth, bible-aligned: locals defending
what's theirs. Never signposted.)

## Composition

South→north: **road** (deploy) → open **fields** with fence lines (the tactical middle —
cover, flanking lanes) → the **waystation** (objective, NE) with **light woods** W (wave-2
cover). One landmark off the road — a **wayside well `[GUESS]`** — as the glitch spot.
Tile vocabulary already proven: grass/dirt road/fence 9-slice/brick building/3×3+single
trees/stone pad.

## Bonding v1 (born here — the system this mission needs)

Minimal **bonds-through-use** per `systems_design.md` (build the accrual; defer the spend):

- `BondSystem` autoload: pair scores keyed `"A|B"`, persisted via PlayerDataManager.
- **v1 sources:** a follow-up fires between a pair (+2, via EventBus/ComboSystem); a heal
  (+1); adjacency at turn end (+1, capped per turn). Downed unit: −2 across their pairs
  (the systems_design "lingering cost" — wire now or defer, small either way).
- **v1 surface:** numbers only (debug/stats panel). No UI art, no thresholds, no duo
  abilities yet — those are the later spine items. The point: after this mission, *how
  you fought* is recorded as relationship state for the first time.
