==========================================
WORLD CONTEXT
==========================================

THE AUTHORITY
-------------
An external empire that has taken root over a long period, building
its own empire through conquest and absorption. The Authority has
been waging a campaign against the kingdom for some time before
the game begins. The war is not new — soldiers on both sides are
veterans. The Authority presents itself as righteous and competent.
Its true motive is the absorption of the Guardian.

The Authority's leadership structure above Commander Vael is TBD.

THE KINGDOM
-----------
An established kingdom centered around the Guardian — a being the
kingdom was built around and sustained by, in the same relationship
as Silvermoon City to the Sunwell. The kingdom's culture, people,
and land are all tied to the Guardian. The Authority is not
conquering the kingdom for territory. They want what the kingdom
was built on.

The kingdom has been resisting the Authority's advance for a long
time before the Hero arrives. By the time of Act 1 the kingdom is
exhausted and losing.

THE GUARDIAN
------------
The protector and source of the world's magic. The kingdom was
built around the Guardian's presence and sustained by it. The
Guardian is the reason the Authority wants the kingdom — not for
territory, but to absorb the Guardian and become a deity figure,
seizing control of the world's magic entirely.

The Guardian foresaw the war and its likely loss. In a last act,
it reached across worlds and summoned the Hero — its final hope.
The Authority detected the ritual and intercepted it mid-summoning,
capturing the Guardian before it could complete the transfer. The
Guardian was blindsided. It had foresight but not omniscience.

The Hero arrives with no memory of the descent or the Guardian's
voice. The Guardian is suppressed by the Authority after capture
but actively fights through that suppression throughout Act 1 —
slipping fragments of contact through as interference, glitched
and incomplete. These slips escalate in frequency and urgency as
the Authority tightens its hold. Vael explains them away as
"artifact interference" — receptive minds picking up bleed from
a dangerous weapon. The Hero has no basis to contradict this.

The Guardian's slips are the only honest voice in Act 1. They
never complete a full sentence until Beat 9.

True nature and appearance: TBD

THE HERO
--------
A silent protagonist with no backstory. Summoned by the Guardian
from another world just before the war reaches its climax. The
Hero arrives with no memory of the descent or the Guardian's voice
— only a feeling that a voice called them here and asked them to
find it.

Vael immediately frames this: the kingdom uses a corrupted artifact
to pull soldiers from other worlds. The voice was the artifact
luring them. The Hero has nothing to contradict this with. They
fight Act 1 believing the voice belongs to someone the kingdom
is holding — someone to rescue, or a weapon to stop. Either way,
a reason to move forward.

The Hero never hears the Guardian clearly until Beat 9. The slips
throughout Act 1 are half-sentences, static, incomplete — enough
to feel familiar, not enough to understand. The player recognizes
the texture of the opening voice. The Hero does not know what to
make of it.

The Hero has a name entered by the player.

THE COMPANIONS
--------------
Six companions join the Hero across Act 1. All are from different
parts of the Authority's empire — they grew up in it, were shaped
by it, and believe in it to varying degrees at the start.

Classes: Archer, Healer, Wizard, Tank, Knight, Rogue

Individual identities, names, regions, and personalities: TBD
Companion groupings for opening sequence visions: TBD

COMMANDER VAEL
--------------
Authority commander leading the campaign against the kingdom.
Presents as warm, calm, and competent to the Hero and party.
Privately views the Hero as an annoyance to be managed — an
asset requiring babysitting. Behind the party's back Vael is
cruel — executes civilians, burns towns, operates without
mercy. Vael knows the Authority stole the Hero to use against
the Guardian and performs warmth to keep them compliant.

Vael does not know the Act 2 party exists at first. Becomes
aware of a shadow cell disrupting operations mid-Act 2 but
cannot identify them. Suspects but does not confirm their
identity until the final confrontation. Dies before reporting
to higher Authority. Never learns the party time-traveled.

Final motivation: TBD

THE UNCHOSEN COMPANION
----------------------
Determined by the Hero's two highest relationship scores at
the moment of the companion choice. The Hero must save one
of the two. The other is left for dead, later rescued by the
Authority off-screen.

The Unchosen Companion does not witness the Authority's true
motive or the Act 1 final battle. They still believe in the
Authority. The Authority fuses with the Guardian and sends
the Unchosen Companion back through time to pursue the
Act 2 party.

In Act 2 the Unchosen Companion is an Authority general and
special forces commander hunting the Hero's party. They
pursue through Act 2, finally cornering the party at the
moment of the original revelation — and witness the Authority
become the monster. They let the party go. They lead their
forces to fight the transformed Authority in the final battle.

Silent reconciliation with the Hero after the final battle.

THE LOVED NPC

==========================================
ACT 1 — THE LOYAL BLADE
==========================================

PREMISE
-------
The Hero awakens in the Authority's summoning chamber with no
memory of the descent or the Guardian. Commander Vael greets
them warmly. The Authority is presented as righteous — invading
a kingdom to claim what they describe as a dangerous artifact.
The Hero trains, forms bonds with companions, and fights for
the Authority across a campaign that ends in the Guardian's
capture. Everything is a lie.

NARRATIVE THROUGH-LINE
----------------------
The Hero fights for the Authority believing the cause is just —
and believing the voice they remember is either a prisoner to
rescue or a weapon to neutralize. Vael provides this framing
immediately and the Hero has nothing to refute it.

Throughout Act 1 the Guardian pushes through the Authority's
suppression in fragments — glitched, incomplete slips that feel
like interference. A half-sentence. A wrong color. A moment where
the world's edge doesn't look right. Vael calls it artifact bleed.
The party accepts this. The slips escalate as the Authority closes
in on the Guardian's location, the suppression becoming harder to
maintain. By the final beats they are frequent and harder to
dismiss — but the party is too deep in the mission to stop.

The Hero never investigates. They trust Vael. They trust the
mission. They are moving toward the voice the whole time without
knowing it.

COMPANION CHOICE
----------------
At a critical moment two of the Hero's closest companions
fall — the two with the highest relationship scores. The Hero
can use a fragmentary Guardian power to save one. The other
is taken by the Authority. This is the Unchosen Companion.

SHADOWED FIGURES
----------------
Throughout Act 1 the party occasionally glimpses cloaked
figures in some battles. Movement unusual. The Authority
labels them enemy saboteurs. The party accepts this.
These are the Act 2 party.

ACT 1 MISSIONS
--------------

NOTE: Mission count, specific locations, and objectives are
TBD pending companion and map design. The following story
beats must be represented across Act 1 missions in order:

Beat 1 — ARRIVAL
  Hero awakens in summoning chamber.
  Vael greets Hero with soldiers and torchlight.
  First impression of Authority: warm, righteous, capable.
  Location: Authority summoning chamber / base camp
  Gameplay: Introductory, no combat yet

Beat 2 — TUTORIAL / TRAINING
  Hero trains with Authority soldiers under Vael's oversight.
  Sparring in progress — controlled, tutorial-paced.
  Mid-sparring: insurgents attack the camp without warning.
  The party repels them. Vael frames this as kingdom agitators
  striking at a soft target — unprovoked aggression.
  First taste of real, lethal combat following the tutorial warmup.
  Companions begin joining the party. Camp life begins.
  Location: Authority training ground
  Gameplay: Two-phase — sparring (tutorial) interrupted by insurgent
             raid (first real stakes combat)
  NOTE: In Act 2 the Act 2 party is inside the camp during this exact
        battle — the loyalist attack is a deliberate distraction they
        sent to pull the Act 1 party outside. The two parties are in
        the same place at the same time, separated by the camp walls.
        The Act 1 party never knows. They hear the same battle the
        Act 2 party is racing against.

Beat 3 — EARLY CAMPAIGN
  Party advances against kingdom defenders.
  Defenders framed as insurgents by Authority intelligence.
  First combat missions against kingdom forces.
  Minor anomalies begin — subtle, easily dismissed.
  Location: Kingdom outskirts / border territories
  Gameplay: Standard campaign missions, party establishing

Beat 4 — SHADOWED FIGURES FIRST APPEARANCE
  Cloaked figures appear in a battle.
  Authority labels them enemy saboteurs.
  Party accepts the explanation.
  Location: TBD
  Gameplay: Standard mission with unusual enemy element

Beat 5 — BRIDGE INCIDENT
  Party witnesses a bridge collapse mid-mission.
  Interpreted as sabotage by unknown enemies.
  Actually destroyed by the Act 2 party.
  Location: Bridge map — mirrored in Act 2
  Gameplay: Mission disrupted by environmental event

Beat 6 — TOWN MISSION
  Party executes an Authority operation in a civilian
  settlement. Casualties occur.
  First moment the cost of the Authority's war is visible
  to the party — framed and rationalized by Vael.
  Location: Civilian settlement
  Gameplay: Mission with civilian presence

Beat 7 — THE CHOICE
  Two closest companions fall — the two with the highest
  relationship scores with the Hero.
  Hero uses fragmentary Guardian power to save one.
  The other is taken by the Authority.
  Location: TBD
  Gameplay: Narrative beat, limited or no combat

Beat 8 — GUARDIAN SECURED
  Authority completes the campaign, capturing the Guardian.
  The kingdom's defense collapses.
  Location: Kingdom capital or Guardian's location
  Gameplay: Final Act 1 campaign mission

Beat 9 — THE REALIZATION
  Guardian manages a final communication hinting at their
  true nature. The Hero begins to understand something is
  wrong.
  Location: Post-battle, Guardian's location
  Gameplay: Narrative beat, no combat

Beat 10 — THE LAST STAND
  Hero attempts to stop the Guardian's absorption ritual.
  The party loses.
  Guardian sends a fragment forward through time.
  The Unchosen Companion is sent forward by the Authority.
  Location: Ritual site
  Gameplay: Unwinnable or narratively resolved battle


==========================================
ACT 2 — THE FRACTURED BLADE
==========================================

PREMISE
-------
The Hero and surviving party arrive before Act 1 begins.
The summoning circle is not yet active. Act 1 events are
inevitable — the timeline must still occur. The party
operates in the shadows, sabotaging the Authority's
campaign from the margins while preparing the kingdom's
final defense. They are the shadowed figures the Act 1
party saw and dismissed.

NARRATIVE THROUGH-LINE
----------------------
The party moves through the same war from the other side.
Every intervention is deliberate — they know what is coming
because they lived it. The Unchosen Companion hunts them
throughout. The same maps, different objectives. The same
war, opposite perspective.

ACT 2 MISSIONS
--------------

NOTE: Mission count, specific locations, and objectives are
TBD pending companion and map design. The following story
beats must be represented across Act 2 missions in order:

Beat 1 — ARRIVAL
  Hero and party arrive before Act 1 begins.
  The summoning circle is not yet active.
  The Guardian's fragment is partially spent — grants
  special powers, the only thing that can reach the
  Guardian if the Authority completes the ritual again.
  Location: Kingdom, pre-war
  Gameplay: Establishing mission, new objectives introduced

Beat 2 — OPERATING IN THE MARGINS
  Party begins sabotage operations across multiple fronts.
  Supply lines disrupted, deployments disrupted, civilians saved.
  Every intervention prepares the kingdom's final defense.

  KEY OPERATION — TRAINING GROUND INFILTRATION:
  The Act 2 party sends a small kingdom loyalist force to attack the
  camp perimeter — enough to pull the Act 1 party and guards outside
  to respond. While the Act 1 party fights the distraction outside,
  the Act 2 party slips into the emptied camp.
  The two parties are in the same place at the same time, separated
  only by the camp walls. The sound of the battle outside is the Act 2
  party's cover — and their clock. They must retrieve the item and
  leave before the fighting stops and the Act 1 party returns.
  They are never seen. They do not interact. They hear their own voices
  through the walls.
  What they retrieve: an item that arrived during the Guardian's
  original summoning ritual alongside the Hero, confiscated by the
  Authority, and never shown to the player in Act 1.
  (What the item is: TBD. Tied to the Guardian's intent.)
  The party passes the summoning circle during infiltration — the
  same one that intercepted the Hero's descent. They recognize it.

  VAEL OVERHEARD — AND THE SUMMONING:
  The Hero has not yet arrived. Vael is not performing. The party
  hears him — through a wall, around a corner, across a courtyard —
  talking to an adjutant or officer. Casual. Not a speech.
  Two subjects, one conversation:
  (1) The kingdom's defense: contemptuous and dismissive. The
      insurgents have been fighting for months and this is the best
      they can manage. Bored contempt — swatting a fly, not fighting
      a war. This is the main thread of the conversation.
  (2) The incoming party (the Act 1 Hero and companions): an offhand
      aside. A band of misfits. He's been told to work with them and
      he finds the assignment beneath him. Not rage — he can't be
      bothered with rage. They're a footnote.
  Specific lines: TBD — writer to draft. The misfits comment should
  feel like the end of a longer thought, not a standalone verdict.

  Vael's contempt is interrupted mid-sentence by the summoning.
  A bright light — the summoning circle activating. The Guardian
  pulling the Hero across worlds, intercepted by the Authority.
  The Act 2 party sees it. They are standing in the camp where
  it happened, watching the moment their timeline began.
  They cannot stop it. The loop must hold.

  Vael's tone shifts instantly. The performance comes back on.
  He goes to meet the Hero. The camp, emptied by the distraction,
  begins to fill again — soldiers returning, attention shifting.
  The window is closed. The Act 2 party must leave now.
  The summoning is the exit signal. They go.
  They carry everything they just heard and saw.

  Vael becomes aware of the shadow cell — cannot identify them.
  Frustration begins building.
  Location: Various — mirrored Act 1 maps; training ground map is
             the same as Act 1 Beat 2, visited at an earlier point
             in the timeline
  Gameplay: Stealth, sabotage, disruption objectives; training-ground
             operation is player-directed — coordinate the loyalist
             raid, then navigate the emptied camp to retrieve the item

Beat 3 — BRIDGE EVENT
  Party destroys the bridge to hinder the Authority.
  The Act 1 party witnesses it and misinterprets it
  as enemy sabotage. The loop closes.
  Location: Bridge map — same map as Act 1 Beat 5
  Gameplay: Destruction / disruption mission

Beat 4 — LOVED NPC RESCUE
  Party rescues the Loved NPC from the fate witnessed
  in Act 1. Hidden safely.
  Identity of Loved NPC: TBD
  Location: TBD
  Gameplay: Rescue mission

Beat 5 — UNCHOSEN COMPANION FIRST APPEARANCE
  The Unchosen Companion arrives as an Authority general
  pursuing the party. First confrontation or near-miss.
  They know the party exists but not yet their identity.
  Location: TBD
  Gameplay: Pursuit / escape mission

Beat 6 — VAEL UNMASKED
  Without Authority framing, Vael's actions are
  transparent. The party witnesses what Vael does when
  the Hero is not watching. A specific scene of Vael's
  cruelty — quiet, routine, not theatrical.
  NOTE: The party already overheard Vael unguarded once in
  Beat 2. This beat escalates from words to action — they
  see what he does, not just what he says when unobserved.
  Location: TBD — observed from shadow
  Gameplay: Narrative beat, possible stealth element

Beat 7 — VAEL'S FINAL CONFRONTATION
  Vael finally identifies the party.
  Recognizes the Hero and all companions — people he
  managed and dismissed, now revealed as the shadow cell
  that dismantled his operation.
  Vael fights out of wounded pride and rage, not strategy.
  Vael dies before reporting to higher Authority.
  Never learns the party time-traveled.
  Location: TBD
  Gameplay: Boss battle — Vael as mid-Act 2 miniboss

Beat 8 — THE REVELATION MOMENT
  The Unchosen Companion corners the party.
  At this exact moment the Authority — fused with the
  Guardian — becomes the monster. The Unchosen Companion
  witnesses it directly. Cannot rationalize it away.
  Lets the party go.
  Location: TBD — mirrors Act 1 revelation location
  Gameplay: Narrative beat, no combat

Beat 9 — FINAL BATTLE PREPARATION
  Party and Unchosen Companion's defectors prepare for
  the final battle. Kingdom's last forces align.
  Location: Kingdom capital approaches
  Gameplay: Preparation / setup mission

Beat 10 — FINAL BATTLE
  Location: Kingdom capital under attack.
  Guardian partially absorbed.

  THREE FRONTS:

  Left Flank — Kingdom
    Last defending army, civilians, rescued Loved NPC.
    Objective: Hold the flank, protect civilians.

  Right Flank — Returned Companion
    Unchosen Companion leading Authority defectors.
    Objective: Break through Authority lines.

  Center — Hero
    Party pushes toward the corrupted Guardian.
    The fragment is the key to freeing them.
    Objective: Reach the Guardian.

  RESOLUTION:
    Fragment reaches Guardian.
    Authority control collapses.
    Guardian freed.
    Hero reaches the intended destination of the
    initial descent — where the Guardian was trying
    to bring them all along.
    Survivors stand together.
    Some wounds healed. Some permanent.

  Gameplay: Three-front tactical battle, largest in game

Beat 11 — SILENT RECONCILIATION
  After the battle.
  Hero and Unchosen Companion.
  No dialogue. A look. A nod. Two people who have been
  through an impossible amount finally standing in the
  same place without fighting.
  The player fills in the rest.
